## Facts to use (all checked in the repo)

- Source: NASA SVS 3912, Global Sea Surface Currents and Temperature; ECCO2 model output,
  25 Mar 2007 – 3 Mar 2008. Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
- Region A: Kuroshio eddies east of Japan, alignment about 0.07, second pulse about 73% of the way
  through the beat ("short–long"). Region B: Equatorial Pacific streaks, alignment about 0.67,
  second pulse about 58% ("more even" than A). (`video_30s.md`, "What the real app says".)
- The two rhythms differ by about 150 ms in where the second pulse lands, at a 1-second beat
  (`docs/ACCESSIBILITY_LOG.md`, "Listening task").
- Neither region is called "even" by the app; use "more even" and "less even".
