## What we know about the rules

Checked on the official pages on 14 Nov. Mr. E re-checks the live form.

- Demo is a video of 30 seconds or less, **or** up to 7 slides including title and intro.
- Demo and project links must open with no password, permission or sign-in.
- AI tools are allowed; the form asks whether and how we used them.
- Every outside resource (code, text, images) is listed in References.
- No copyrighted music or media without permission.

Not yet confirmed: exact field names, language rules for the video, rules for participants under 18.

## Rules for every draft

1. **Write only what the app really does.** If it is not working at Sunday 2 PM, take it out.
2. **Three honesty lines go in every piece:**
   - It is a visualization of a computer model (ECCO2), not a photo or direct measurement.
   - The rhythm rule is a choice we made.
   - It describes the rendered image, not water speed, turbulence or ocean health.
3. **Real numbers or none.** Count what happened. Keep our own checks separate from outside feedback.
4. **Privacy.** No student names, faces or voices anywhere public. Use "Student A" and "Student B".
   Video shows hands and screen only. Use captions instead of a student's voice.
5. **Credit.** "NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio" is visible in the app, video and slides.
6. **Do not use the NASA logo** as if NASA made or approved the project.
7. **AI help is fine and is logged.** If an AI tool helped you write a draft, add a line to `AI_USE.md`, then read every sentence and fix anything that is not true.

## How to hand in a draft

Edit your file on a branch, open a pull request, and tell your reviewer. When the reviewer
approves, change `Status: draft` at the top of the file to `Status: reviewed by <role>`.
