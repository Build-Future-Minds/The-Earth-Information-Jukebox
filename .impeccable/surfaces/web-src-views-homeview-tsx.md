# Earth Sonata Home (landing page, #home)

Scope: the Home view, `web/src/views/HomeView.tsx`, shown when the address has no `#`. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Space Apps judges and first-time visitors, including blind, low-vision and keyboard-only people.
Job: in a few seconds, know the problem, the idea and what to press; then find every page and the NASA sources.
Task: docs/page_plan.md, P0. Constraints: PRODUCT.md honesty lines and credit; no sound before a press; NASA stills and the app's own drawings only; no tester numbers; roles, not names; same tokens, fonts and themes as the app (DESIGN.md, Tide Table).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is the first sheet of the same tide-table chart: the problem is set as the chart's title, the NASA picture sits in its graticule frame with both regions lettered, and everything after is ruled rows. It refuses the generic product page: no hero card, no feature-card grid, no stats.

OWN-WORLD: Tide Table unchanged: almanac white or night navy, navy ink, tide-blue primary action, violet A and rose B with their letters, the dark chart well behind drawings, 1px hairline section rules, 4px corners, no shadows, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with mono for numbers and SVS ids only.

STORY: The visitor reads the problem (data locked in pictures), the idea (patterns become sound by checkable rules), sees where the two regions are, then the rule in three pictures, the five steps with links, quick links to every page and NASA source, and what the sound does not tell them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1440 x 900, left 5/12: the problem as the h2 (first sentence lighter, second in full ink, about 2.4rem), the idea at 1.35rem, then the one tide-blue "Start listening" link-button with the Listen icon and the line "Nothing plays until you press Play." Right 7/12: the ocean picture in its chart frame with both rings equal, credit directly under it, a key naming A and B. The top of "The idea in three pictures" shows above the fold.

FORM: Tide Table, inherited from DESIGN.md (the app's world); no roll, because the page plan pins the sections and the picture beside the opening text. seed: none (unavailable). Signature: the five steps and the honesty rows set as a ruled tide table, each row a link or a fact.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
