How the rhythm is made

Lines that run the same way give alignment near 1: two evenly spaced pulses per beat. Lines that run every which way give alignment near 0: long–short. Rule: the second pulse lands at 0.75 − 0.25 × alignment of the beat.

In this game, pitch and note length also follow alignment, and Stretch widens the spacing. The team's rule keeps pitch fixed. Numbers here are a quick browser measurement, not the team's. It describes the picture, not water speed or ocean health.
